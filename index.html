About

Passionate about designing interactive experiences, focusing on experimental and nontraditional ways to tell immersive stories.

I study Digital Narrative and Interactive Design at the University of Pittsburgh. Most of the game work I have done is within the Unity engine, experimenting with 2D, 3D, and VR. Outside of games, I have experience with video editing and animation.

For me, games are but one avenue to tell a story. Medium changes based on what will work best to tell a particular narrative, so I try to not limit myself to focusing in on games alone. Open to experimenting on various technical and creative interactive projects.
